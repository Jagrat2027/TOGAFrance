# Requirements Specification – School Traffic Light

## a) Brief Summary

Detailed list of functional and non-functional requirements to ensure project success.

## b) Requirements Gathering Method

- Public meeting with families (06/15/2025)
- Consultation with technical staff
- Review of regulatory framework

## c) Structured List of Requirements

| ID     | Requirement                           | Type           | Priority  | Validation           |
| ------ | ------------------------------------- | -------------- | --------- | -------------------- |
| EX-01 | Push-button traffic light             | Functional     | Mandatory | Technical test       |
| EX-02 | Audible signal for PRM                | Non-functional | Desired   | PRM user feedback    |
| EX-03 | Power consumption < 50 kWh/year       | Non-functional | Mandatory | Supplier measurement |
| EX-04 | Installation outside school hours     | Organizational | Mandatory | Validated schedule   |
| EX-05 | Easy-to-read instructions             | TOGAFrance     | Desired   | Parent review        |
| EX-06 | PRM accessibility                     | Legal          | Mandatory | Compliance check     |
| EX-07 | Backward compatibility (easy replace) | Technical      | Optional  | Supplier review      |
| EX-08 | Crossing time suited to PRM          | Functional     | Mandatory | Test with PRM user   |

EX-08 was added on 01/09/25 after feedback from a family with reduced mobility (monitoring log, deliverable 14): this is the ADM requirements-management loop.

## d) Associated Validation Criteria

- Test with families and PRM users present.
- Power consumption measured after 1 month.
- Feedback report after 6 months.

## e) Risk Analysis (Non-Compliance)

- Equipment delivery delay.
- Non-compliance with accessibility.
- Lower user satisfaction if no audible signal.

## f) Stakeholders Consulted

- 3 technical staff, 12 families, PRM representative, council member.

## g) Change History

- 2025-07-07: First draft after public meeting.
- 2025-09-01: EX-08 added after feedback; identifiers added for traceability.
